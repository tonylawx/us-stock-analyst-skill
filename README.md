# US Stock Analyst Skill

Codex skill for writing and iteratively improving evidence-led Chinese US stock daily analysis. It supports both fresh-source days and news-sourced original days when no fresh update exists.

The skill lives in `us-stock-analyst/`.

## Research & Content Engineering Discipline

Rooted in institutional equity research discipline and **Industrial Content Engineering Methodology**:

- **Source hierarchy**: strict separation of primary facts, company statements, consensus estimates, reporting, and interpretation.
- **Five-phase section progression**: advancing substantive sections through *Tape Facts $\to$ Micro-Ledger Penetration $\to$ Conflict & Incentive Mapping $\to$ Historical Analogies $\to$ Decision Pivots*.
- **Claim chain**: `Conclusion → Evidence → Mechanism → Trading implication`.
- **Sector micro-accounting toolkits**: concrete unit economics and cash flow conversion for AI hardware/semis, enterprise SaaS, and advanced manufacturing.
- **Two-turn cognitive hook**: moving beyond superficial news to ledger reversal and systemic physical/capital deadlocks.
- **Half-tree principle for news days**: single-tension core spine to prevent fragmented news collages.
- **Road transition curves**: connective logic bridges ensuring smooth paragraph flow.
- **Price-attribution confidence**: rigorous grading (high / medium / low) without forced percentage pseudo-precision.
- **Event scenario tree**: balanced bullish/neutral/bearish game plans with explicit invalidation points.
- **Light pre-publish research gate**: enforcing analytical rigor before downstream publication.

## Privacy & Portability

This repository is intended to remain reusable and user-agnostic. Do not add personal biography, account-specific portfolio details, private source names, local filesystem paths, credentials, private workflow identifiers, or other identifying context. When a useful rule comes from a private case, keep only the generalized analytical rule.
