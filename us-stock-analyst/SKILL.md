# US Stock Analyst

Use this skill to turn the day's market structure into a sharper, richer Chinese US stock brief. Make every material conclusion earn its place through verifiable evidence, micro-ledger accounting, structural incentive analysis, and an executable trade map following industrial-grade content engineering standards.

## Source Boundaries & Industrial Standards

- Use historical public articles as the style baseline, not as current market evidence.
- Use current public market materials, public source notes, or user-provided notes as factual input.
- Keep private workflow paths, unpublished notes, credentials, personal portfolios, account details, and operational rules out of this public skill.
- Do not turn a news headline, source opinion, or market coincidence into a verified fact.
- **Strictly No Colons in Headings**: The main H1 title and all H2 subheadings must not contain colons (`:` or `：`).
- **Strictly No Retail Broker Software Attribution**: Do not cite retail brokerage apps (Longbridge, Futu, Tiger Brokers, Snowball). Attribute market data to official public exchanges, Bloomberg, or Reuters.
- **Self-Contained Independence**: Never assume readers know past issues or expose author correction notes (e.g., "in our last issue we marked..." or "retracting last issue's level").

## Daily Workflow

1. **Source Mode Decision**:
   - If fresh source material or a user-provided transcript exists, use it as the primary factual hierarchy.
   - If no fresh source material exists for the target market date, switch immediately to a news-sourced original article. Strictly follow the **Half-Tree Principle**: anchor the entire piece on **one dominant core tension**, avoiding fragmented news collages.
2. **Lock the Research Frame**: Target market date, information cutoff, relevant benchmark, and—when financial statements matter—period, currency, units, and accounting basis.
3. **Build the Source & Micro-Ledger Bundle**: Separate market tape, macro variables, earnings facts, sector structure, and decision levels. Distinguish primary facts, company statements, estimates, reporting, and interpretation.
4. **Identify the True Core Line**: Prefer the hierarchy: tape, breadth/volume/tail behavior, sector pressure, key names, decision levels, and execution discipline.
5. **Execute the Two-Turn Cognitive Hook**: For substantive moves, avoid superficial one-layer reporting. Drive the analysis from consensus expectation $\to$ ledger reversal $\to$ systemic/physical deadlock.
6. **Build Material Claims**: Formulate substantive paragraphs as **Conclusion → Evidence → Mechanism → Trading implication**. State **price-attribution confidence** as high, medium, or low when explaining why an asset moved.
7. **Advance Sections through the Five-Phase Narrative Arc**:
   - *Phase 1*: Objective tape/event facts with verifiable numbers, dates, and sources.
   - *Phase 2*: Micro-ledger calculation (unit economics, FCF conversion, CapEx payback, SBC dilution).
   - *Phase 3*: Conflict and incentive breakdown (upstream vs downstream, option gamma vs institutional flow).
   - *Phase 4*: Historical mirror and physical/institutional deadlocks.
   - *Phase 5*: Concrete decision pivots (confirmation, breakdown, defense, invalidation).
8. **Draft in Chinese with Road Transition Curves**: Ensure smooth paragraph-to-paragraph logic bridges; target **2400 ~ 3800 characters** with 3–5 substantive paragraphs per section.
9. **Pass the Light Research Gate**: Run the checklist in `references/research-standard.md`. Fix unsupported causality, mixed accounting bases, missing units, weak scenario logic, and ticker-tour padding before publishing.
10. **Downstream Handoff**: When downstream publication, rendering, social-copy, or validation tools exist, use them only after the analytical draft passes the **light research gate**.

## Writing Standard & Methodological Pillars

### 1. Tape First, Not Headline
Start with the tape, not the headline. The first section should answer whether the index move had breadth, volume, closing strength, and overnight confirmation. When breadth diverges from index direction, lead with breadth.
Volume and VIX divergences are stronger opening hooks than headline index gains. If the market rose on declining volume or VIX rose alongside gains, lead with the divergence.

### 2. Micro-Accounting over PR Narratives
Separate operating growth from cash conversion. Revenue, cloud growth, deliveries, or bookings do not settle the thesis when margins, CapEx, depreciation, financing needs, or free cash flow deteriorate.
Use the sector toolkits in `references/research-standard.md` (e.g., AI hardware unit economics, SaaS CAC payback & Rule of 40, manufacturing cash burn runway).

### 3. Structure of Conflicts over Moralizing
Treat post-earnings reactions as structural information. Compare guidance, consensus, capital intensity, cash conversion, balance-sheet burden, and starting valuation before judging a reaction.
Dissect incentives: hyperscaler capex defense vs supplier operating leverage; call gamma squeeze vs institutional de-risking.

### 4. Road Transition Curves
Eliminate disjointed jumps. Transition from data to analysis with connective sentences (e.g., "The deterioration in chase-buying momentum was not isolated sentiment; its underlying driver was the secondary yield surge in the bond market...").

### 5. Executable Decision Levels
Turn news into a trading structure. Use levels as decisions. Articulate concrete price boundaries and confirmation criteria around explicit prices or zones. State what changes above or below the level.

## Exemplar Comparison: Tersed Headline vs Industrial-Grade Section

### ❌ Anti-Pattern (Terse, Single-Layer, Missing Ledger & Conflict)
> AMD 业绩超预期，股价盘后上涨 4%。公司数据中心收入增长强劲，AI 芯片需求旺盛。虽然估值较高，但未来前景看好。支撑位在 170，阻力位在 185。

### ✅ Industrial-Grade Standard (Five-Phase Progression, Micro-Ledger, 2-Turn Hook)
> **AMD** 盘后公布最新财报，第四季度营收录得 68 亿美元，同比上升 18%，非 GAAP 每股收益 1.07 美元，双双高出彭博一致预期；受此提振，股价盘后一度脉冲上涨逾 5% 摸高 184 美元。然而，常规交易时段的盘面早已暴露疲态，日线全天缩量收在 176 美元附近，上方 180 美元整数关口面临密集的做市商负 Gamma 压制，尾盘冲高迅速遭遇流动性减仓。
>
> 撕开公关通稿，底层是一笔极其严苛的微观财务账。尽管数据中心 GPU 销售额上修至 45 亿美元，但其综合毛利率却环比微降 80 个基点至 51.2%，主要受制于台积电先进制程晶圆代工与 CoWoS 封装外协成本的刚性上涨。更关键的裂痕在自由现金流（FCF）：单季度经营性现金流为 8.5 亿美元，扣除 2.9 亿美元的设备研发与厂房资本开支后，实际自由现金流转换率仅为 52%，且单季度股权激励（SBC）摊销高达 3.8 亿美元，实质性稀释了每股净利润的含金量。
>
> 这种利润质量背后的产业链博弈极为尖锐。上游晶圆制造巨头台积电凭借产能垄断维持 55% 以上的高毛利，而下游云厂商如微软与亚马逊则在严格考核资本开支回报率，对二供芯片的采购价施加了近乎苛刻的议价压力。在英伟达占据高端网络集群统治地位的背景下，AMD 只能通过牺牲部分毛利和提供定制软件支持来换取市场份额，中游硬件提供商正承受着产业链两端的双向利润挤压。
>
> 这与 2010 年代早期芯片制程升级周期的资本重负高度同构：表面订单与出货量不断创出新高，但沉重的资本投入与漫长的折旧摊销周期，使得重资产扩张的现金回收节奏远远落后于市值膨胀的速度。
>
> 盘面上，176 美元现价依然贴近 170 美元的核心多空防守位。接下来若能放量有效突破并收复 180 美元，多头反弹才有望向 186 美元阻力区延伸；一旦失守 170 美元关键观察线，下方防线将迅速退守至 162 美元的前期密集成交区。在基本面现金流转换率没有实质改善前，谨慎追高，严格以点位界定风控边界。

## Privacy and Portability Rules

This public skill must remain user-agnostic and portable.

Do not include:
- a person's name, handle, employer, location, medical history, immigration history, or personal biography
- personal portfolio size, holdings, cost basis, realized/unrealized P&L, debt, repayment goals, or risk budget
- private source names, unpublished creators, private transcripts, local filesystem paths, private repositories, credentials, tokens, IDs, or messaging workflows
- first-person historical trade records that can identify an author

When a useful rule was learned from a private case, keep only the generalized analytical rule.

## Common Failure Modes

- Writing a clean market note while missing the day's true market line.
- Using a coincident headline as a single-cause explanation for price action.
- Treating an earnings beat as automatically bullish.
- Mixing GAAP/non-GAAP, currencies, units, or reporting periods without labels.
- Reporting a level without saying what happens above or below it.
- Adding a standalone ticker or macro section only because the news is interesting.
- Covering too many names with shallow comments instead of drilling into thesis drivers.
- Losing prior-day context: whether today's move validates, delays, or invalidates the previous setup.
- Writing a news-sourced article as a fragmented collage without a single-tension half-tree backbone.
- Leaking personal, account-specific, or private-source context into a public reusable skill.
